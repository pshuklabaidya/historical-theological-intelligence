# Whole-work, developmental and comparative cartography

Use cartography only for the requested macro question. Ordinary translation retains its existing route, depth and completion. Identity licenses, textual witnesses, proposition logic, historical relations, explanation records, confessional relations and cartographic aggregation remain separate.

## Evidence and corpus

Save one self-contained `<TASK_DIR>/hti-cartography.json` document with `schema_version: hti-cartography-1` and four lists: `datasets`, `corpora`, `maps`, `comparisons`. IDs are unique within each list. All lower-level objects are existing native evidence records, never prose substitutes or remembered facts. Every included dataset preserves its actual work, edition and exemplar licensing level.

Each dataset has exactly `id`, `identity_checkpoint`, `identity_binding`, `metadata`, `integrity`, `source_registry`, `dossier`, `uncertainty_request`, `historical_graph`. Reuse the public source-integrity, Argument Dossier, uncertainty and Historical Relation Graph contracts. `historical_graph` is null when unused. Existing multi-work HRGs resolve their nodes against the independently validated dossiers and uncertainty analyses embedded in the document. A referenced historical edge must actually touch the observation’s current dossier/propositions. The general identity checkpoint and binding must pass `specialization_core.identity_license` independently of specialization contents. `integrity` is the complete current Source-Representation Integrity registry; its native PRG hash and proposition set must match `dossier`. Use separate datasets for multiple dossiers or works. Do not put another work's dossier into the current work dataset.

Metadata has exactly `author_id`, `genre`, `audience`, `period`, `source_form`, `source_base`, `availability`, `date`, `evidence`. The selected general work identity claim also records `author_id`, matching the metadata author. Metadata values require inspected source references; differences must remain visible. Date has `subject` (`WORK_COMPOSITION`, `WORK_PUBLICATION`, `EDITION_PUBLICATION`, `TRANSLATION_PUBLICATION`, `UNRESOLVED`), `state` (`SECURE`, `UNCERTAIN`, `UNAVAILABLE`), `earliest`, `latest`, `evidence`, `rationale`. Years are integer bounds, or both null for unavailable dating. Secure dates require positive inspected dating evidence at the recorded subject level. Edition dates require independent edition identity. Translation-publication dates cannot order original authorial development. Metadata and date evidence belong to that dataset's work. A source reference has exactly `dataset_id` and `ref`; `ref` is the existing argument-integrity `{representation_id, span_ids, snapshot}` reference. Translation may represent argument sense; it cannot license exact original terminology or eliminate translator intervention.

Each corpus has exactly `id`, `work_id`, `state`, `boundary`, `inventory`, `evidence`, `rationale`, `limitations`. `state` is `SUFFICIENT_WHOLE_WORK`, `SECURE_PARTIAL`, `INCOMPLETE` or `UNRESOLVED_BOUNDARY`. `boundary` names the actual inspected or intended universe; positive corpus evidence and a reasoned sufficiency assessment remain mandatory. Inventory portions have exactly `id`, `status`, `dataset_ids`, `rationale`. Portion status is `INSPECTED`, `UNINSPECTED` or `UNAVAILABLE`. Only inspected portions have evidence datasets. One portion ID denotes one underlying passage even when there are multiple representations. Unavailable material contributes no occurrence and no negative evidence. Whole-work sufficiency requires every inventoried portion inspected. Every other corpus state requires explicit limitations. Unresolved boundaries limit macro confidence to possible or unresolved. Never declare sufficiency merely because every conveniently available passage was inspected.

## Observations, significance and claims

Each map has exactly `id`, `corpus_id`, `dimensions`, `observations`, `claims`. Each dimension has `id`, `definition`, `coverage` (`INSPECTED`, `NOT_INSPECTED`, `UNAVAILABLE`), `rationale`. Explicitly record missing dimension coverage. Dimensions include doctrine, exegesis, authority, distinctions, Scripture, theological structure, interpretive habits, argumentative patterns, structural roles, pastoral/political applications, polemical concentration, source transmission, confessional relation, terminology and doctrinal placement. The exact enum values are exported as `DIMENSIONS` by `scripts/hti_core/cartography.py`.

An observation has exactly `id`, `dimension`, `value`, `role`, `portion_id`, `dataset_id`, `proposition_ids`, `relation_ids`, `historical_refs`, `source_refs`, `rationale`. Its dataset must belong to an inspected corpus portion. Proposition ownership, current source snapshots and native dossier content are checked. Authorial doctrine requires an author assertion or qualification. A cited authority or Scripture premise may support it only with its actual quoted ownership, native authority function and a licensed supporting edge to that authorial proposition. Opponent and unresolved voices cannot become authorial doctrine. Every proposition must intersect its cited source evidence. `relation_ids` name native logical relations with endpoints among those propositions. Roles are `OCCURRENCE`, `STRUCTURAL_DEPENDENCY`, `INTEGRATION`, `TRANSITION`, `LOCAL_INTENSITY`, `BOUNDED_DISTRIBUTION`, `BOUNDED_CONTRAST`, `PERIPHERAL_FUNCTION`. Structural roles require appropriate native logical relations. Distribution and contrast require a reasoned bounded evidentiary assessment, never a synonym for count. A confessional observation records a doctrinal relationship and cannot create a historical edge.

Historical references have exactly `kind`, `id`. `EXPLANATION` references current Stage 8 consumers with their complete HRG and uncertainty bindings. These support local polemical concentration and local intensity only. They do not establish centrality, development or dependence. `HISTORICAL_EDGE` references an already validated HRG edge for source-transmission mapping. Preserve its actual relation type and confidence. The aggregate creates no historical edge. An authority citation alone cannot become immediate source dependence.

Each map claim has exactly `id`, `dimension`, `observation_ids`, `scope`, `label`, `significance`, `text`, `confidence`, `limitations`, `status`, `snapshot`. Scope is `WHOLE_WORK` or `BOUNDED_CORPUS`. Whole-work scope requires a sufficient whole-work corpus and multiple underlying passages. Source roots and passage IDs are deduplicated, including derivative representations. Claim confidence is qualitative: secure, probable, possible, unresolved. Status is `CURRENT` or `REVALIDATION_REQUIRED`. No numerical confidence or universal importance score is accepted.

Significance records have `id`, `state` (`SUPPORTED`, `QUALIFIED`, `UNRESOLVED`), `observation_ids`, `rationale`. Separate `DOCTRINAL_CENTRALITY`, `FORMULATION_FREQUENCY`, `CONFIGURATION_FREQUENCY`, `STRUCTURAL_ROLE`, `LOCAL_POLEMICAL_INTENSITY`, `CROSS_LOCUS_INTEGRATION`. Frequency licenses frequency only. Centrality requires structural dependencies across loci; integration requires cross-locus structural connections. Local controversy licenses local intensity only.

Label is null, `central`, `representative`, `exceptional`, `integrative`, `transitional` or `peripheral`. Each requires its actual source-bound role. Central also requires supported doctrinal centrality and structural role. Representative requires bounded distribution across passages; exceptional requires bounded contrast within the map. Conflicting significance dimensions require qualified confidence and explicit limits. Exceptional conveys no novelty, first occurrence, historical rarity or originality.

## Dated development and structural comparison

A comparison has exactly `id`, `kind`, `dimension`, `definition`, `sides`, `finding`, `attribution`, `confounds`, `alternatives`, `asymmetry_assessment`, `text`, `confidence`, `limitations`, `status`, `snapshot`. Side has `map_id`, `claim_ids`. Every side selects current source-bound claims on the same explicitly defined, inspected dimension. Missing evidence never becomes negative evidence.

`COMPARATIVE` uses `STRUCTURAL_SIMILARITY`, `STRUCTURAL_DIFFERENCE` or `UNRESOLVED`, with attribution `STRUCTURAL_COMPARISON_ONLY` and empty confounds. The renderer retains corpus size, completeness, unavailable portions, genres, periods, source forms and dimension coverage. `asymmetry_assessment` states how those differences constrain the comparison. Structural comparison cannot adjudicate dependence, genealogy, reception, common source, novelty or originality.

`DEVELOPMENTAL` uses `OBSERVED_CHANGE` or `UNRESOLVED`, with attribution `AUTHORIAL_DEVELOPMENT`, `OBSERVED_DIFFERENCE_ONLY` or `UNRESOLVED`. Every dataset must independently license the same author. Authorial development requires securely dated, non-overlapping ranges in side order. The permitted changing dimensions are terminology, authority, exegesis, distinction, doctrinal placement, polemical qualification and argumentative pattern. Confounds include `GENRE`, `AUDIENCE`, `LOCAL_CONTROVERSY`, `SOURCE_AVAILABILITY`, `SOURCE_BASE`, `EDITION`, `TRANSLATOR`, `EDITOR`, `COVERAGE`, `OCCASION`. Each has `id`, `state` (`CONTROLLED`, `LIVE_ALTERNATIVE`, `UNRESOLVED`), inspected `evidence`, `rationale`. Control must explain why the factor cannot account for the observed change. Edition or translator/editor difference cannot become authorial development without positive original-source attribution evidence. Live competing explanations remain in `alternatives`, with qualified confidence and limits. Uncertain dates permit comparison of differences but cannot force a developmental sequence.

## Revision, current publication and completion

Macro snapshots bind the claim's selected propositions and logical relations, source spans and ancestry, uncertainty, identity licenses, historical/explanation references, corpus inventory and dimension definitions. Comparisons additionally bind selected map claims, metadata and dating. Any material correction requires revalidation. A persisted record with an old snapshot cannot remain current. Stale records can be retained as `REVALIDATION_REQUIRED`, and cannot be rendered as current.

No automatic specialization write or activation is added. A map may be retained as external work research, with its full evidence and fingerprints, under the existing work specialization `argument_research` surface. Reconstruct and validate the current evidence before using it. Retention never establishes identity, restores stale claims, licenses another work, or promotes work evidence to edition/exemplar evidence.

The public validation and rendering interface is:

```python
from scripts.hti_core.cartography import Cartography, revalidate
# record contains the exact source-bound document described above.
# Use revalidate only after explicit evidence review and correction.
record = revalidate(record)
validated = Cartography(record)
outputs = validated.current_outputs()
```

Save the reviewed `record` to `<TASK_DIR>/hti-cartography.json`. For every selected current output, add one evidence checkpoint claim with `claim_type: cartography`, `cartography_id` equal to the output key, and `cartography_sha256` equal to SHA-256 of the exact UTF-8 output text. Put each exact output into a separate response-plan block through the existing claim-text interface, retaining ordinary paragraph separation between outputs. Register actual named referents through the inherited typed entity interface. Preserve source-body coverage and all inherited output bindings. The cartographic dataset for the current task must contain the exact current `source-integrity.json` object and support a current map of that task’s work. Every current comparison must include that map. Another work’s current map requires its explicit participation as a comparison side; an unused copy of the current dataset cannot license unrelated outputs.

For each substantive follow-up use the existing `task_state.py begin-publication` operation, prepare current evidence, then `stage-publication`, and complete with the existing commentary helper. The helper checks every selected current cartographic output, hash, evidence license and current staging; it owns the final response and receipt. No cartography record appearing after staging is accepted. No alternate renderer or completion bypass is provided.

Novelty Detection, Novelty Ledger, rarity/originality ladders, earliest occurrence and negative historical search claims remain unavailable at Stage 9.

## Current accountable publication

The common `publication_contract` and response-plan digest in [output templates](output-templates.md) govern this feature's substantive output and selected-operation outcome. A retained result is not fulfillment of a new request. Publish selected current exact projections or reviewed current derivations; disclose a current PARTIAL/UNRESOLVED/BLOCKED reason when a selected operation cannot be completed. Preserve native source, ownership, uncertainty, identity and relation licenses. Unrequested operations remain optional. All research, source inspection, corrections, preparation and progress updates precede the committed response. Design R selects transport from exact UTF-8 bytes: DIRECT_STDOUT at or below 24,000 bytes emits the committed answer and stops with no later tool; READ_COMMITTED_FILE above that ceiling emits a short current-publication path/hash/byte-count stub, followed only by one exact Read with allow_large=true, exact relay and stop. No other post-stub tool, narration, editing or new publication is permitted. Later truthful recovery preserves the same commit and repeats its direct payload or bound stub, never scholarship.

Only the publication/completion adapter changes: scope.domain_outputs chooses exact current map/comparison output IDs and required current operation outcomes. Retained unselected maps do not require repeated visibility or satisfy new work. Native dataset/whole-work/corpus/dating/confound/aggregation/comparison semantics are unchanged. An unknown page anchor is never a licensed Cartography dataset.

Selection review: multiple authors or source works alone do not request comparison. Developmental cartography uses MAP_DEVELOPMENT with its full prerequisite closure; add COMPARE_WORK_MAPS only when the user actually requests comparison. Unestablished development remains a governed UNRESOLVED outcome, not a reason to substitute comparison.
