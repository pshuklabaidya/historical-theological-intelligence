# Pastoral Study

Base pastoral study on the translated passage, not on a generic topic. Give: the passage's main claim; exegetical movement; two or three doctrinal observations; pastoral uses; cautions or historical distance; and questions for study or preaching.

Clearly distinguish the author's position, confessional comparison, and present application. Do not hide an uncertain reading because it makes a smoother lesson. If the decisive Latin is unresolved, condition the application on the possible readings.
