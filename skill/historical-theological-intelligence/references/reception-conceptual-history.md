# Reception and Conceptual History

Use this reference only for explicit bounded reception, mutation, or conceptual-history analysis.

## Evidence boundary

- Resolve antecedent and recipient to validated argument, component, or proposition references.
- Preserve the bounded passage, proposition, distinction, component, or cluster scope.
- Require an active Phase 5 `RECEPTION`, `TRANSFORMATION`, `REVIVAL`, or `REJECTION` edge when claiming historical reception.
- Permit conceptual comparison without genealogy, but label it `CONCEPTUAL_COMPARISON_ONLY`.
- Preserve Phase 6 lineage gaps and Phase 7 transmission or immediate-source status.

## Independent dimensions

Compare lexical form, propositional content, semantic scope and field, distinction structure, argument function, doctrinal context, authority structure, and application function separately. Do not force lexical and conceptual continuity to agree. Do not use numerical similarity or confidence scores.

Reception may retain, reformulate, narrow, broaden, relocate, reject, revive, combine, or abandon bounded material. More than one mutation may apply. Shortness does not prove narrowing, length does not prove broadening, contradiction does not prove rejection, and absence does not prove abandonment.

## Historical categories

Separate historical terminology, reconstructed conceptual content, later analytical categories, and later analogies. A later label never becomes an earlier actor's self-description. An analogy never establishes reception, identity, or dependence.

## Translation and transmission

Keep translation as representation separate from translation as historical event. Attribute translator- or intermediary-induced change to that event unless evidence establishes a recipient's own conceptual mutation. A modern translation cannot create historical lexical or conceptual evidence.

## Output

Build a structured candidate with `reception_history.py`. Validate all references, evidence roots, reception licensing, mutation records, comparisons, counterevidence, competing interpretations, uncertainty, and provenance before rendering a bounded profile. The analysis never creates or changes a Phase 5 edge.

Phase 9 confessional registry and locus tracing, novelty or originality adjudication, hidden-interlocutor reconstruction, polemical pressure fields, and whole-work cartography remain unavailable.
