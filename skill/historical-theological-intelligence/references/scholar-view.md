# Scholar View production contract

Scholar View is the inherited v0.1 scholarly-translation operation. The supplied page remains the controlling observational witness. Keep diplomatic Latin, normalized Latin, English translation, witness evidence, external research, and inference distinct. Never fill unreadable page text from another witness.

## Minimal execution path

1. Read the supplied page and produce the complete English translation. Use the full-resolution supplied image as the default inspection surface; read [image processing](image-processing.md) before any derivative and register finite regions for consequential evidentiary questions. Every view starts from the original; close the question RESOLVED or UNRESOLVED before completion.
2. For an image anchor, make one diplomatic transcription and one normalized Latin layer. Run `python3 scripts/complete_scholar_view_task.py --prepare-long-s --state-dir "$HTI_STATE_DIR"`. The helper derives all ordinary s/f token state from both layers and reports only cross-layer mismatches or unaligned s/f-sensitive tokens as requiring manual review. Inspect those reported cases against the supplied image, correct the transcription/normalization itself, and rerun until `manual_review_required=0`. Preserve visible `ſ`, genuine `f`, and round/final `s`; mark any unresolved form uncertain rather than forcing a reading. Do not hand-edit deterministic checklist IDs, ordering, or resolution categories.
3. If external research is available and not declined, attempt one useful independent comparator. Inspect the comparator passage itself. Once one adequate comparator and enough discriminating evidence for the justified identity state have been obtained, stop searching unless a conflict or material uncertainty requires another source. A catalog hit, OCR snippet, contents page, or the supplied commentator's own statement about a variant is not an independent witness.
4. Verify only sources that materially support the requested Scholar View. One failed lookup does not abort the page.
5. Record only page-supported theological loci and exegetical-method observations. Historical description remains distinct from later confessional comparison.
6. Fill `<TASK_DIR>/scholar-view-task.json` from `resources/templates/scholar-view-task.json` and run `python3 scripts/complete_scholar_view_task.py --state-dir "$HTI_STATE_DIR"` from the installed Skill root. Do not hand-build the canonical checkpoint or response plan during routine Scholar View. The adapter writes both canonical files and delegates to the canonical audit and renderer.

## Compact task rules

After general identity assessment, run `python3 scripts/complete_scholar_view_task.py --prepare-resources --state-dir "$HTI_STATE_DIR"` before specialized witness research. This builds the general identity checkpoint and eligible installed context from the compact task, without publishing an answer or claiming research persistence. Use a stable bibliographic `work_id`; the generated work claim binds that subject. Independently admitted identity evidence must retain `origin: general_evidence`, `ancestor_evidence_ids` and current status as required by [specialization core](specialization-core.md). Edition/exemplar claims in `task.claims` additionally retain their independently established `subject_id` and `origin: general_identity`.

For the licensed work, follow [licensed resources](licensed-resources.md), including the automatic targeted historical-translation search when alignment is possible. If preparation fails, fix the general identity evidence or continue general HTI explicitly without specialized state. Existing native/imported or used state requires reconciliation and cannot be overwritten. Update the compact task with research results, then prepare long-s review and complete normally. Identity corrections after resource preparation require fresh revalidation; do not reuse the old binding. Preparation metadata is not the final answer.

`translation` contains the complete English page translation.

`sections` uses fixed keys. In analytical sections, quotation marks are reserved for literal supplied-page wording; write English glosses or paraphrases unquoted unless they are carried by a licensed analytical claim.
 For image tasks, routine required keys are `rare_book_notes`, `diplomatic_latin`, `normalized_latin`, `textual_uncertainties`, `theological_loci`, and `exegetical_method`. `textual_uncertainties` may be an empty list when no concrete non-foreign uncertainty remains; the adapter supplies the canonical foreign-reading status and a neutral empty-state sentence when needed. `witness_comparison` is required through the explicit witness status. `source_notes`, `notes`, `citation`, and `research` are optional.

`witness_comparison.status` is:

- `completed` when at least one independent comparator passage was inspected. Supply its evidence ID(s). Comparator evidence must be `other_witness` or `historical_translation` and record `passage_inspected: true`.
- `unavailable` when research was attempted but no comparator passage could be inspected. Give a short limitation.
- `declined` when the user declined external research. Give a short limitation.

For `completed`, record `witness_comparison.comparisons[]` with `supplied_text` copied from the diplomatic or normalized transcription, `comparator_text` copied from the independent comparator, its `evidence_id`, and `characterization` (`orthographic_only`, `substantive_difference`, or `unresolved`). The comparator evidence records the exact `inspected_passage` containing its excerpt. Any claim that an entire passage differs only orthographically requires `comparison_scope: entire_claimed_passage`, `claimed_passage` copied from the supplied transcription, every comparison characterized `orthographic_only`, and a full match against each comparator evidence `inspected_passage`. The helper rejects that characterization if the recorded word sequence differs after conservative spelling normalization. The worker must still identify material syntactic or semantic differences; the helper does not decide philological equivalence. A partial comparison must be described as partial.

For an actual supplied-image anchor, the adapter automatically supplies `E-SUPPLIED-PAGE` as the supplied-page observation. Additional `evidence` items use the ordinary evidence shape: `evidence_id`, `evidence_class`, `kind`, `independence_group`, `description`, and, when relevant, `provenance`, `identity_admissible`, and `passage_inspected`. The template carries the complete compact contract in `_interface`; `python3 scripts/complete_scholar_view_task.py --show-interface` prints the same nested field requirements, role/scope pairs, long-s ownership rules, examples, and accepted enums. Final completion begins with an aggregate preflight that reports independent current blockers together where dependencies permit; repair the whole reported batch before rerunning rather than discovering each blocker serially. Promoted work identity accepts `bibliography`, `other_witness`, or `supplied_image_observation` evidence classes plus admissible provenance-bearing discriminating kinds. Invalid enum diagnostics list accepted values directly.

For a named established work, set `identity.state` to `WORK_ESTABLISHED`, provide `identity.name`, `external_verification`, and admissible `identity.evidence_ids`. Identity evidence must carry provenance, `identity_admissible: true`, and a discriminating kind such as `explicit_author_title`, `distinctive_phrase_match`, `page_level_text_match`, `sequence_match`, `textual_bibliographic_match`, `full_text_passage`, `explicit_imprint_colophon`, `distinctive_running_title`, or `verified_dossier_fact`. Do not establish identity from style, theology, genre, memory, or a generic running head. Leave edition and exemplar unresolved unless separately established.

Register genuine non-current historical names, source works, institutions, and comparator works once in `entities`. Resolve named displays inside a governed passage before its review; bind the matching entity IDs in that assertion. Standalone entity markers do not publish a sentence. Legacy markers inside high-risk analytical claims are resolved by that existing authoring adapter before the common reviewed payload is bound. Common role/scope pairs are: `historical_name` with `page_content`, `historical_source`, or `other_noncurrent`; `historical_source` with `historical_source`; `external_comparator` with `external_comparator` plus a distinct `target_work_id`; `external_bibliographic_record` with `external_bibliographic_record` plus a distinct `target_work_id`; `scripture_reference` with `scripture`; and `other_named_entity` with `other_noncurrent`. Every entity needs supporting `evidence_ids`. A visible page token does not itself identify a historical person: when the displayed referent differs from the visible form, record `identification_status` as `explicit_page_name`, `independently_verified`, `inferred`, or `unresolved`. Use `page_name_form` for `explicit_page_name`; use the visible `page_token` plus `identification_evidence_ids` for `independently_verified`; use the visible `page_token` that prompted an inferred/unresolved referent. Inferred or unresolved referents may render only through an inference or unresolved analytical claim. Do not repeat the current work's own author/title in analytical prose; renderer-owned Identification handles it.

`foreign_language_tokens` binds any explicitly analyzed Greek or Hebrew form to its source layer, reading status, and any proposed gloss. Diacritic-only alternatives belong in `alternate_forms` under the same token ID when the underlying letters are identical; a different letter sequence is a different reading/lexeme and is not an alias.
 When the underlying letters are secure but breathing/accent remains uncertain, `alternate_forms` may hold diacritic-only alternatives under the same token ID; every alternate must reduce to the same underlying letter sequence as the primary form. Use literal UTF-8 text rather than hand-entered Unicode escapes. Separate `reading_evidence_ids` from `gloss_evidence_ids`: reading evidence establishes the form or glyph, while gloss evidence establishes meaning. A supplied-page token must include `E-SUPPLIED-PAGE` in `reading_evidence_ids`, occur in the diplomatic or normalized layer, and be checked against the image. A translation may support the gloss but does not establish the supplied-page glyph. If a gloss is supplied, record `gloss_status`, `gloss_evidence_ids`, and `gloss_checked_against_token: true`. An inconsistent pair fails closed. A probable, uncertain, or unresolved token may not appear as unqualified analytical prose; the adapter also appends a renderer-derived count of non-secure foreign-language readings to Textual uncertainties so structured uncertainty cannot remain hidden.

Every substantive analytical assertion, including ordinary theological synthesis, must be an exact canonical projection, reviewed current derivation or governed unresolved statement. A coherent bounded passage may use one reviewed derivation. Legacy high-risk analytical_claims retain their additional source/token/entity checks and require a matching governed assertion; selected_claim_ids remains identity-only.
 Each record has a `claim_id`, `claim_type`, `status` (`supported`, `inference`, or `unresolved`), `text`, and evidence as appropriate. Insert it with `{{claim:CLAIM-ID}}` as the actual claim phrase/sentence; do not place the marker in parentheses after a paraphrase because it expands to the full claim text. Registered `{{entity:ENTITY-ID}}` markers are permitted inside claim text and are resolved before named-referent validation; multiple referenced entities are retained as `entity_ids`. The adapter writes these records into the canonical claim list. The renderer, not model-authored claim text, adds `Inference:` and `Unresolved:` labels, preventing renderer labels from re-entering the named-referent scanner. Ordinary theological synthesis does not need an analytical-claim record merely because it is analysis.

For rare-book notes, state visible features directly and place any claimed function in an analytical claim. The adapter also rejects high-confidence explicit function language such as “used for,” “serves as,” “functions as,” “keyed to,” or “cross-referencing” when it appears as untyped plain rare-book prose; render such wording through a typed `feature_function` claim.
 Observation that a marginal letter is present is page evidence. A claim about what the letter does is an inference unless its function is separately established. Apparent photographed size does not establish bibliographical format such as folio or quarto, and visible board wear does not establish binding history.

Preserve visible abbreviation and suspension marks in diplomatic Latin, including Latin Extended and combining-mark forms; do not expand a visible diplomatic abbreviation merely to satisfy token alignment. `long_s_review.checklist_file` may be omitted; the helper uses the checklist in the selected task directory and rejects cross-task paths. The checklist is not model-authored state. `--prepare-long-s` aligns diplomatic and normalized Latin, derives context-bearing token IDs and ordinary `long_s`/`genuine_f`/`round_s` categories, and reports only s/f-sensitive cross-layer mismatches or unaligned tokens for manual visual review. Correct the source text itself and rerun until no manual review remains; then set `reviewed_against_supplied_image: true`. The prepared checklist stores hashes of the exact diplomatic and normalized Latin. Unrelated non-Latin edits do not require manual renumbering or re-resolution. Any Latin edit requires `--prepare-long-s` again; final completion fails closed on a hash mismatch or on any remaining s/f mismatch and identifies the exact diplomatic/normalized pair requiring image adjudication.

## Completion behavior

If the adapter reports a compact-task error, fix the compact file and rerun the same command. Final completion begins with an aggregate preflight that reports independent current blockers together where dependencies permit; repair the whole reported batch before rerunning. The template, `_interface`, and concise diagnostics expose the routine schema and accepted values; implementation-source inspection is not part of the normal repair path.

The adapter uses the shared final publication operation after canonical validation. It saves the exact response and input-bound receipt, then emits its selected transport once. Never redirect the publishing helper or use it as a captured test. All research, source inspection, corrections, preparation and progress updates precede the committed response. Design R selects transport from exact UTF-8 bytes: DIRECT_STDOUT at or below 24,000 bytes emits the committed answer and stops with no later tool; READ_COMMITTED_FILE above that ceiling emits a short current-publication path/hash/byte-count stub, followed only by one exact Read with allow_large=true, exact relay and stop. No other post-stub tool, narration, editing or new publication is permitted. Later truthful recovery preserves the same commit and repeats its direct payload or bound stub, never scholarship. A Continue/resume turn does not weaken the terminal contract: substantive output requires governed current completion or verified same-response transport recovery.

If the runtime or tool limit stops before helper success, preserve the structured files and make the entire assistant message exactly:

`Structured completion is unfinished. Continue to resume from the preserved checkpoint.`

## Scholar View from supplied text

Keep the scholar route for a non-image request. Register the actual primary text in source-integrity.json and set compact source_anchor to {"representation_id":"ACTUAL-ID","evidence_id":"PRIMARY-ID"}; bind that evidence ID on its representation. The adapter supplies that primary evidence item, so do not duplicate it in task.evidence. Keep locus.page_id and image_sha256 null. Set sections.supplied_text to the exact registered content; it displays in Notes, with the actual representation layer and unavailable image apparatus disclosed through current governed assertions. Do not label normalized supplied text photographed diplomatic Latin. Non-image tasks omit diplomatic_latin and rare_book_notes; normalized_latin is permitted only for an actual NORMALIZED_TRANSCRIPTION anchor. Required non-image sections are supplied_text, textual_uncertainties, theological_loci and exegetical_method, with the same witness status discipline.

The selected SCHOLARLY_TRANSLATE outcome is PARTIAL with actual current results, a visible specific reason and limitations for unavailable image-dependent apparatus. COMPLETED would falsely complete that apparatus; total BLOCKED would discard useful work. Preserve the full translation, gaps and uncertainty. ORIGINAL_TEXT retains its own permissions; RAW_OCR and derivatives remain provisional unless genuinely reviewed; historical/modern translations cannot establish underlying wording or glyphs. Primary text or its derivative is not an independent comparator. Explicitly analyzed Greek/Hebrew from supplied text uses source_layer supplied_text and its primary reading evidence, never a false image check.

For actual image tasks retain exact locus/hash and E-SUPPLIED-PAGE binding, diplomatic and normalized separation, prepare-long-s, real image attestation, high-risk gates and finite registered inspection. For non-image tasks prepare-long-s reports inapplicability without manufacturing a checklist; structured completion remains mandatory and all core source permissions still apply.
