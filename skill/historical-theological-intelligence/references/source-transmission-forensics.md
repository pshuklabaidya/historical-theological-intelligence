# Source-Transmission Forensics

Use this reference only for an explicit immediate-source, intermediary, common-source, edition-use, or Source-Transmission Profile request.

## Required distinctions

Keep named authority, ultimate antecedent, immediate source, intermediary or channel, shared source, and access channel separate. A citation does not establish direct consultation. Historical reachability does not establish immediacy. Chronology and accessibility are conditions, not proof of use.

## Structured workflow

1. Bind the question to a validated proposition and bounded material scope.
2. Register candidate immediate-source, intermediary, edition, translation, and shared-source hypotheses.
3. Bind every feature to inspected representations, spans, evidence roots, provenance, inspection status, and verification status.
4. Keep OCR, metadata, catalogue, index, snippet, semantic-search, embedding, n-gram, and sequence-similarity results as leads until relevant passages are inspected.
5. Check chronology, accessibility, counterevidence, alternatives, and shared evidence ancestry.
6. Inherit only relevant Phase 4 uncertainty.
7. Adjudicate without forcing a winner. `PREFERRED` means best among inspected alternatives, not established.
8. Require Phase 5 validation before establishing or changing a historical relation edge.
9. Serialize a bounded Source-Transmission Profile when requested.

## Fail-closed rules

- Common agreement, shared biblical language, and widespread distinctions are not discriminating by themselves.
- Modern translation resemblance cannot establish source-language dependence.
- OCR error cannot become historical corruption.
- Several features from one root are not independent corroboration.
- Access without discriminating evidence does not establish use.
- Failure to locate an intermediary does not establish direct consultation.
- Failure to locate a source does not establish independence or originality.
- A passage-level conclusion does not generalize to a whole work.
- Comparator evidence never overwrites the supplied page, witness state, identity, dossier, or graph state.

Phase 7 does not implement systematic reception or conceptual-history analysis, confessional tracing, novelty adjudication, or full natural-language routing. Ordinary translation remains ordinary translation.
