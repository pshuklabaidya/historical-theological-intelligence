# Licensed installed resources

General HTI identifies a work without consulting specialization contents. After that assessment, automatically prepare eligible installed resources before substantive specialized interpretation. No user toggle is required. Unidentified and candidate sources continue through general HTI; no resource content is read. Author identity or shared vocabulary never licenses another work. The identifier catalog is routing metadata, never bibliography or identity evidence.

Record a general identity binding in `<TASK_DIR>/identity-binding.json`: `scope` contains independently established `work_id`, `edition_id` and `exemplar_id` (unresolved levels are null); `claims` maps each established level to its selected general identity claim ID. The claim's `subject_id` is the stable bibliographic identifier already established by general evidence. Catalog identifiers designate objects; they cannot establish that the supplied source is one of those objects. Consult the generic catalog only after identity establishment to bind an already identified object; never use its contents to promote identity. Work ID `WORK-VERMIGLI-JUDGES` designates Peter Martyr Vermigli's *In librum Judicum commentarii*. Edition ID `EDITION-VERMIGLI-JUDGES-ZURICH-1561` designates its independently established 1561 Zurich edition. Neither identifier is an identity discriminator.

Run `python3 scripts/specialization_resources.py prepare --state-dir "$HTI_STATE_DIR"` after general identity assessment and before specialized interpretation. The common adapter derives the unique selected identity itself for commentary or Scholar View. An optional `--binding` must equal that independently derived binding. For Scholar View it derives only general identity from the compact task through the existing identity builder; it does not publish a response. The legacy Scholar preparation wrapper remains an unfinished preparatory operation and is not a readiness certificate.

The adapter preserves the primary context, including native/imported registries, receipts and existing uses. Its sibling `installed_readiness` carries a separate immutable-resource task snapshot. The four outcomes are `DORMANT`, `VERIFIED_NO_ELIGIBLE`, `PREPARED` and `UNAVAILABLE_AFTER_VALIDATED_ATTEMPT`. Dormant tasks open no catalog. No eligible resources requires authenticated exact catalog matching. A real missing/unreadable required file after a bounded attempt can permit honest general fallback; identity, path, schema or hash failures are hard failures. Never claim an unknown catalog means no eligibility.

The isolated installed snapshot is `INSTALLED_READ_ONLY`, not native recovery, portable research or a persistence write. The adapter writes only owned task state; no account discovery, root bootstrap, backend or installed-Skill mutation occurs. Existing primary state requiring identity reconciliation must be explicitly reconciled under existing rules before refresh. Used installed state cannot be erased or silently replaced to conceal stale claims.

After exact work licensing, record `vermigli-judges-work` exposes resource `historical-translation`. After independent edition licensing, `vermigli-judges-1561` exposes `bibliography`. Read a licensed packet with `python3 scripts/specialization_resources.py read --state-dir "$HTI_STATE_DIR" --record RECORD --resource RESOURCE`. Packets retain claim-level scope, provenance, inspection basis, caveats and source ancestry. Bibliographic reference facts are not evidence that licenses their own activation. No exemplar record is installed; physical-copy facts require separate actual evidence under the existing exemplar machinery.

## Historical translation comparison

After licensing the exact Judges work, Scholar View automatically performs a targeted search of the registered historical English translation where the supplied passage permits alignment. Ordinary translation remains controlled by supplied Latin; historical comparison is added when useful or requested. Run `python3 scripts/specialization_resources.py search --state-dir "$HTI_STATE_DIR" --record vermigli-judges-work --resource historical-translation --query "distinctive English phrase"`. Search another discriminating idea when necessary and inspect enough context from both sources. A failed or uncertain alignment remains explicit. English syntax alone does not establish mistranslation; qualify comparison until direct clause-level inspection supports it. No full witness dump is required.

Every CLI read/search rechecks general identity and resource hashes and invalidates the publication draft and readiness seal. Select `--view installed` or `--view primary` internally when record IDs coincide; ambiguity is a hard failure, not implicit registry merging. Results retain exact file/line locators and facsimile markers. Register inspected excerpts in the task's existing Dynamic Witness Graph and source-integrity registry as `HISTORICAL_TRANSLATION`, language English, shared root/independence group `HISTORICAL-TRANSLATION-A68840`, with provenance noting derivation from the Latin work. Preserve the English witness's own printer/date identity separately from the supplied Latin edition. Every hit, page block and repeated quotation belongs to ONE translation witness. Its translation relation to the Latin is derivative, not independent Latin corroboration. Page markers are extraction locators, not silently licensed Latin foliation.

The supplied Latin controls its visible readings. Historical English never overwrites it, becomes exact Latin evidence or supplies a universal fallback. The existing source-integrity `permit` gate and proposition/coverage machinery govern represented argument, quotation, uncertain voice and reception use. The flattened extraction may have lost apparatus or non-Latin text: do not infer secure ownership or completeness from it. Trace any proposed original wording to the original-language witness. Do not impose a fixed witness hierarchy.

## Completion and correction

Use installed reference claims through `specialization_core.read_claim`. `specialization_resources.bind_use` returns the exact context use and an output claim; append that claim to the evidence checkpoint only after general identity has been established. Its specialization origin must remain visible in structured state. Primary uses remain primary and installed uses remain in their isolated snapshot. `bind_use` accepts an internal `owner` when necessary and invalidates the seal in its returned context. Output IDs must be unique across both views. The common terminal gate authenticates current package bytes, identity and each owning use before claim/output binding; it does not prepare, search, attach or write readiness during completion. The same completion receipt binds `specialization-context.json`. No freeform completion bypass is added.

Changed licensing evidence requires revalidation of the affected scope; corrected edition identity does not change a work-level license. Reprepare only before any use, or explicitly reconcile stale used claims through the existing revision machinery. Source-derived research claims continue to use span-sensitive FOUNDATION dependencies and the external registry. Installed reference claims do not incorporate task OCR, annotation or proposition findings. General translation, historical routing, source verification and rare-book work remain available without specialization or persistence.
