# Westminster-Confessional Comparison

Compare the page with the Westminster Standards only when requested. Use the Phase 9 registry and proposition-level workflow in [confessional-creedal-locus-tracing.md](confessional-creedal-locus-tracing.md). First state the author's bounded proposition from the supplied page, then resolve the exact Westminster document, version, locator, and proposition.

Do not claim historical influence from doctrinal resemblance. Keep doctrinal relation, historical transmission, and theological judgment separate. Westminster is not the automatic endpoint or interpretive key. Prefer a few strong connections over an exhaustive list.
