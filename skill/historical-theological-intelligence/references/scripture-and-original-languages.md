# Scripture and Original Languages

Identify explicit quotations, close allusions, and topical echoes separately. Record the author's printed reference, the modern reference, and the text actually compared.

For Greek or Hebrew:

- transcribe only visible characters that can be supported;
- record font damage, breathing, accent, pointing, or direction uncertainty;
- compare a suitable critical or historical text only as an external witness;
- explain the grammatical or lexical argument in context;
- distinguish the early-modern author's claim from a modern evaluation.

Do not reconstruct Greek or Hebrew from the expected biblical verse without labeling it as reconstruction. Transliteration may aid the reader but does not replace script-level evidence.
