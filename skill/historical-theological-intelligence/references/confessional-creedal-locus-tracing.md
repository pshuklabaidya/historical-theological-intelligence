# Confessional-Creedal Locus Tracing

Use the production registry in `resources/confessional-registry/initial-registry.json` and the bounded locus descriptors in `resources/confessional-registry/loci.json`. Resolve aliases to one stable registry ID. Preserve the registered textual form, version, translation, provenance, and native locator kind.

## Workflow

1. Identify the bounded theological locus and anchor proposition.
2. Select only materially relevant registry documents.
3. Resolve each document's version or translation and inspect the direct text.
4. Decompose the relevant locator into propositions where needed.
5. Apply source-form licensing before wording-sensitive claims.
6. Compare bounded propositions and classify the doctrinal relation.
7. Retain qualifications, counterevidence, competing interpretations, and uncertainty.
8. Check chronology and accepted historical evidence only when dependence is claimed.
9. Reference Phase 8 conceptual analysis where terminology or concept continuity matters.
10. Serialize a bounded comparison or locus trace deterministically.

## Doctrinal relations

- `DOCTRINAL_CORRESPONDENCE`: substantial bounded agreement without a genealogy claim.
- `QUALIFICATION`: limitation, condition, clarification, or specification without necessary contradiction.
- `DISTINCTION`: differentiation of elements another bounded formulation treats together.
- `DEVELOPMENT`: fuller articulation without superiority, originality, inevitability, or automatic dependence.
- `CODIFICATION`: formal creedal or confessional expression without invention or first-occurrence claims.
- `EXCLUSION_OF_ALTERNATIVE`: positive textual exclusion, never silence.
- `TENSION`: a concrete harmonization pressure short of formal contradiction.
- `CONTRADICTION`: incompatible matched propositions after scope, terminology, and qualification checks.
- `LATER_ANALOGY`: useful later comparison without backward influence.
- `HISTORICALLY_DEMONSTRATED_DEPENDENCE`: only a reference to separately licensed historical evidence.

Keep these relations separate from Phase 8 mutation types and Phase 5 historical edges. A strong doctrinal match does not create an edge.

## Text and absence safeguards

A translation may support doctrinal content at the level it licenses. It does not establish exact source-language wording. Surface any material revision, recension, or translation difference.

Distinguish explicit formulation, qualification, exclusion, and contradiction from no located statement, textual silence, search incompleteness, unavailable source, unresolved relevance, and non-relevance. Do not convert any of the latter states into denial or rejection.

## Boundaries

Do not force all registry documents into every trace, rank confessions, create a linear progress narrative, infer originality, aggregate whole-work theology, implement Phase 10 language hardening, or add work-specific specialization.
