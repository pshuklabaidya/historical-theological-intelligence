# Argument capability

Only the kernel may validate and publish the final response and receipt. Native constructors and typed state remain subject to the bound composition.

`Translate this and tell me what the argument is.` adds a bounded validated [Argument Dossier](../../references/argument-architecture.md). Build a supported argument before tracing it; validate historical relations before lineage or transmission; establish a locus before confessional tracing. Dynamic Witness Graph, Proposition-Relation Graph, and Historical Relation Graph remain distinct. Translation in English, Latin, Greek, Hebrew, German, French, or Dutch stays ordinary translation unless further analysis is requested.
